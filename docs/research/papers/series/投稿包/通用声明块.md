## 一、数据可用性声明 / Data Availability

**中**：本研究的基础数据（Levin 词表候选筛选表、996 义位原始义项总表、义位筛选规则表、语料搭配证据及图式得分、域系初判表、跑范畴英汉义位编码与聚类表）均存于课题组数据仓库，处理脚本可重跑复现。因部分数据包含 OALD10 与《现代汉语词典》（第 7 版）的词典原文，受版权限制，公开版本仅保留 SenseID、标签与统计层；确因研究需要访问完整数据者，可与通讯作者联系【邮箱】协商获取。

**EN**: The underlying datasets (the Levin candidate screening table, the 996-sense master table, the sense-screening rule table, the collocational evidence and schema scores, the domain–family pre-mapping tables, and the RUN-category coding and clustering tables) are archived in the project data repository, and all processing scripts can be re-run for reproduction. Because portions of the data reproduce dictionary text from OALD10 and the *Modern Chinese Dictionary* (7th edition), the publicly shared version retains only SenseIDs, labels and statistics; requests for full data access may be directed to the corresponding author 【email】.
